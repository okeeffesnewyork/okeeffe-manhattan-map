Later Manhattan — An Epilogue

O’Keeffe made New Mexico her permanent home in June 1949, but Manhattan continued to appear in her life. She returned in 1950 to install the final exhibition at An American Place, photographed the Chrysler Building through a Waldorf Astoria window around 1960, showed new paintings at the Downtown Gallery in 1961, and helped install the Whitney retrospective in 1970.
